Status: Draft / vaulted synthesis
Date: 2026-04-26
Source: GPT synthesis from Convenor answers + Grok v0.2

## Purpose

DeveloperTraceProvenanceKernel v0.3 reframes the earlier developer-trace marketplace into an architecture/concept provenance and bounty-escalation system.

The goal is to detect potentially valuable work, ask for consent, preserve context, route promising candidates through valuation and human review, and enable compensation where appropriate.

This is not a raw-code surveillance market.

## Core Flow

```text
user creates idea / architecture / solution
  -> AI detects possible value
  -> prompt is calibrated by urgency and confidence
  -> user consents to further analysis
  -> system preserves context / provenance
  -> multi-AI review and valuation
  -> HITL provenance team review
  -> possible bounty, contract, revshare, or drop decision
```

## What Is Captured First

Phase 1 focuses on:

- architecture summaries;
- module concepts;
- implementation plans;
- solution narratives;
- problem / solution mappings;
- valuation analyses;
- enterprise challenge responses.

Phase 1 does not center:

- raw code diffs;
- continuous IDE telemetry;
- private enterprise source code;
- defense / bio / high-risk traces without verified professional context.

## Consent Triggering

Medium-confidence and high-confidence triggers are allowed.

Prompting should vary by urgency:

- Low urgency: “This may be worth preserving or revisiting.”
- Medium urgency: “This may have meaningful value; would you like to analyze it further?”
- High urgency: “This appears potentially high-value or time-sensitive; would you like to start a provenance review?”

## Enterprise Mode

Enterprise mode should support problem-specific bounty toggles.

```text
enterprise states problem
  -> employees use AI to develop solutions
  -> AI flags promising architecture / solution concepts
  -> employee consents to provenance review
  -> enterprise and employee may share bounty / upside
```

This reframes provenance as internal innovation infrastructure, not uncontrolled trace capture.

## Bounty Bootstrap

Initial bounties may be small but motivational:

```text
$100-$500 exploratory bounty range
```

Funding source is TBD and may depend on implementation:

- foundation / Atlas exploratory pool;
- buyer partner pool;
- originating platform;
- enterprise internal bounty pool;
- shared marketplace pool.

## Marketplace Direction

Direct bidding remains preferred for mature versions.

The first buyer class is likely AI training dataset demand, especially novel architecture and code-adjacent reasoning.

Non-exclusive licensing is the key value multiplier: a validated concept or training-set category may be more valuable when multiple model families can bid under consent and governance constraints.

## Hybrid KPI Model

Value should include economic and non-economic impact:

- profit;
- cost savings;
- training efficiency;
- water savings;
- power savings;
- civic benefit;
- environmental stewardship;
- model improvement;
- deployment feasibility.

The goal is not to optimize pure profit while externalizing stewardship.

## Governance

Required gates:

- ConsentKernel;
- PreFlightGate;
- House 12 review;
- HITL provenance team;
- kill switch / full shutdown capability;
- no bundling with access to tools;
- no silent trace absorption.

## Short Form

Start with architecture and concept provenance. Let AI surface hidden value. Ask consent before preserving or escalating. Pay small bounties to prove legitimacy. Delay raw-code trace marketplaces until IP, HITL, and governance are mature.
