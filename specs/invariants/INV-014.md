# INV-014: Zero-Knowledge Where Possible

## Statement

Prefer zero-knowledge proofs for identity verification and data sharing when technically
feasible.

## Rationale

Zero-knowledge proofs allow one party to prove knowledge of a secret without revealing
the secret itself.  Applied to identity (e.g., "I am over 18") or data sharing (e.g.,
"my salary is in this range"), ZKPs eliminate unnecessary data exposure, directly
supporting INV-016 (Data Minimization) and INV-020 (No Silent Sharing).

## When to Apply

This invariant is **advisory**: it requires a best-effort assessment at design time
rather than a hard block at merge time.  Engineers must document why ZKP is or is not
applicable for each new data-sharing feature.
